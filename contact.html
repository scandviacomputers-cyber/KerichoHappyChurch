What to expect on a Sunday

No pressure, no dress code, no awkward “stand up and introduce yourself” moment. Just come.

1Arrive

Come as you are

Arrive about fifteen minutes early. Someone at the door will welcome you and help you find a seat.

2Worship

About 90 minutes

Worship, prayer, notices, a Bible message, and time to respond. Children go to Happy Kids after worship.

3Stay

Chai afterwards

Hang around. Meet a few people. There is no hurry and there is always tea.
