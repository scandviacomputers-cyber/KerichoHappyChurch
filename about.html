Who we are

A Christ-centred family in the heart of Kericho - Kenya

We are ordinary people held together by an extraordinary Saviour — learning to follow Jesus honestly, together, right where God has planted us.

Our story

From a handful of believers to a church for the city

Kericho Happy Church began with a small group of families praying in a living room, convinced that God wanted a joyful, Bible-teaching church at the centre of this town.

What started around a few chairs has grown into a congregation that gathers every Sunday for worship, opens the Word midweek, gathers in life groups across the estates, and carries the gospel into the markets, schools and tea estates of Kericho County.

We are still the same church at heart: happy in Christ, hungry for His Word, and hopeful for our city.

What we believe

Our statement of faith

We are a Pentecostal church — standing on the full gospel, the Word of God and the present power of the Holy Spirit.

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God — our final authority for faith, doctrine and daily living.

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

We believe in the deity of Jesus Christ, His virgin birth, sinless life, atoning death, bodily resurrection, ascension, and personal return in power and glory.

We believe salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone — not by works, so that no one may boast.

We believe in the baptism in the Holy Spirit as an experience for every believer, empowering us for witness and service, and that the gifts of the Spirit — including speaking in tongues — are for the Church today as they were at Pentecost.

We believe in divine healing through the atoning work of Christ. We pray for the sick and trust God to answer.

We believe the Church is the body of Christ, called to worship, to make disciples, and to be salt and light in the world.
