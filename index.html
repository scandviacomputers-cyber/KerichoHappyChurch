You are welcome here

Come as you are.
Leave changed by grace.

Whether you have walked with God for forty years or you are stepping into a church for the very first time, Sunday at Happy Church is for you. Expect warm people, honest worship and a message from the Bible you can carry into Monday.

The Word, plainly taught

We open the Bible every week and let it speak — practical, Christ-centred teaching that meets you in real life, in English and Kiswahili.

Worship that lifts Jesus

Spirit-led, joyful worship where the whole church lifts its voice together. Come early, stay late, and let the presence of God meet you.

A family, not a crowd

Real friendship happens in small circles. Join a life group, serve on a team, and find people who will walk with you through every season.

Our heart

Built on Christ.
Built for the city.

Kericho Happy Church exists to make Jesus known across our town and beyond — in the estates, the markets, the campuses and the tea farms. We believe a healthy church blesses the city it lives in.

Everything we do rests on four simple commitments: know God through His Word, grow together in genuine community, serve our neighbours with practical love, and go with the gospel wherever He sends us.
